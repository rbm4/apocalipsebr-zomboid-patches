# Zombie Population Performance Analysis (11k zeds)

## Architecture confirmed
The client-authoritative model is real: clients simulate zombies they own ([IsoZombie.updateInternal](cci:1://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/characters/IsoZombie.java:3247:4-3503:5) at `@/c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/characters/IsoZombie.java:3248`) and the server's job is authority arbitration + relevance-based relay ([zombie/popman/NetworkZombieManager.java](cci:7://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombieManager.java:0:0-0:0), [zombie/popman/NetworkZombiePacker.java](cci:7://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombiePacker.java:0:0-0:0)). That means **server cost scales with `zombies × connections`, not just `zombies`**, and that's exactly where your 11k-zed spike is landing.

## Root cause

The server does not have a spatial index between "which connections care about which zombies." Every hotspot below is a **linear scan of the full zombie list, crossed with the full connection list**, executed every tick (or every 2s). At low zombie counts this is invisible; at 11k it becomes the dominant cost, independent of [ServerLOS](cci:2://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/network/ServerLOS.java:15:0-428:1) (which you already parallelized and which scales with *players*, not zombies).

## Hotspots (ranked by impact)

### 1. [NetworkZombieManager.updateAuth](cci:1://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombieManager.java:59:4-147:5) - O(zombies × connections × players)
`@/c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombieManager.java:60-148`

Called once per zombie every server tick from [NetworkZombiePacker.updateAuth()](cci:1://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombiePacker.java:153:4-160:5) (`@/c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombiePacker.java:154-161`). When a zombie has no owner or its 2s ownership lease expired, it does a **nested loop over every connection, then every player on that connection** (`NetworkZombieManager.java:110-124`), computing `getRelevantAndDistance` for each. With 11k zombies and even 4 connections/players, that's tens of thousands of distance calcs on top of everything else, every tick that any batch of zombies' 2s lease expires. This is the single biggest suspect for your degradation curve - it's the "orchestrate upward" ownership-reassignment cost and it is `O(N·C)`, not amortized by any spatial structure.

**Fix direction:** invert the loop. Precompute a spatial grid of "which connections/players are relevant to which grid cells" once per tick (you already have `connection.getRelevantRange()` and player positions), then look up candidate owners for a zombie in O(1)/O(k) instead of scanning every connection. This is also embarrassingly parallel across zombies since the only shared mutable state is `owns.lock`.

### 2. [NetworkZombiePacker.getZombieData](cci:1://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombiePacker.java:162:4-198:5) - O(connections × zombiesProcessing)
`@/c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombiePacker.java:163-199`

For **every connection**, it walks the *entire* list of zombies that sent an update this tick (`this.zombiesProcessing`, line 182) and does a relevance-distance check per zombie to decide relay. This is the classic MP sync fan-out: `O(P × Z_active)`. Unlike the client->server send path ([NetworkZombieSimulator.send](cci:1://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombieSimulator.java:286:4-357:5), capped at `MAX_ZOMBIES_PER_UPDATE = 300`, `@/c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/popman/NetworkZombieSimulator.java:36`), this server-side relay loop has **no cap** on the inner scan.

**Fix direction:** this per-connection loop is independent across connections (no cross-connection mutation besides read-only `zombiesProcessing`) - parallelize it across a thread pool exactly like you did for [ServerLOS](cci:2://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/network/ServerLOS.java:15:0-428:1). Longer-term, bucket `zombiesProcessing` by grid chunk so each connection only scans zombies in nearby chunks instead of the global list.

### 3. [ZombieGroupManager](cci:2://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/ai/ZombieGroupManager.java:28:0-311:1) rally "spread out" logic - O(groups²)
`@/c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/ai/ZombieGroupManager.java:88-184`, specifically the inner scan at `ZombieGroupManager.java:116-129`.

Every group leader in `ZombieIdleState`, on its 30-tick cycle, scans **every other group** to compute separation. Group count scales with `zombies / rallyGroupSize`; at 11k zombies this can be hundreds to low thousands of groups, making this `O(G²)` per rally cycle. [findNearestGroup](cci:1://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/ai/ZombieGroupManager.java:185:4-209:5) (`ZombieGroupManager.java:186-210`) is the same O(G) scan, called whenever an ungrouped zombie needs assignment.

**Fix direction:** bucket groups by z-level + spatial cell (a simple hashmap keyed by chunk coords) so leader separation and [findNearestGroup](cci:1://file:///c:/Users/x316739/Downloads/apocalise-zomboid-patches/apocalipsebr-zomboid-patches/42.20.0/decompiled/zombie/ai/ZombieGroupManager.java:185:4-209:5) only check groups in nearby cells.
